Kuruvo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

1. Who we are

Kuruvo ("the app") is an expense-tracking app for Android. The person responsible for the data described in this policy (the "controller") is:

Oğulcan Özkan, an individual developer ("we", "us")
Contact: tembrisstudio@gmail.com

We have not appointed a data protection officer. This policy gives you the information that Article 13 of the EU/UK GDPR requires. The Turkish version of this policy is also the notice required by Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

2. Summary

  • Kuruvo has no account, no sign-up and no cloud sync. Your expenses, categories, budgets, receipts and settings are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. We cannot see them.
  • We run no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no advertising ID in the app, and we do not track you.
  • Payments are made to Google through Google Play. The purchase is between you and Google. The app checks on your phone that your own subscription is valid. We do not run a payment or subscription server.
  • The 7-day free trial that every installation gets is counted on your phone only: the date it started is stored on the device and is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Exchange rates are downloaded from Frankfurter, and only after you have seen the privacy notice on the first screen. The request shows the service your IP address and your default currency.
  • Receipt scanning is off by default. If you switch it on, the text recognition runs on your phone with Google ML Kit. Your photo and the text read from it are not uploaded, but ML Kit sends Google technical metrics that cannot be switched off while scanning is on (section 4).
  • Reminders and alerts are local notifications made by your phone. No push-notification service is used.

6. Transfers outside your country

Google operates worldwide. Frankfurter and its content-delivery provider and GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Turkey. For EEA and UK users, this means that your IP address and, with receipt scanning on, the ML Kit metrics may reach countries without an EU adequacy decision for the recipient. We do not sign standard contractual clauses with Google for ML Kit metrics or with Frankfurter, and we do not claim a specific transfer tool for them. The providers describe their own safeguards in their own policies (links in section 4).

For users in Turkey: there is no adequacy decision under Article 9 of Law No. 6698 (as of 29 October 2026 the Personal Data Protection Board had not made one), and we claim no Article 9 transfer tool for the ML Kit metrics, the exchange-rate request, GitHub Pages or the mailbox provider. We reduced these flows instead: ML Kit is used only after you switch scanning on, and the exchange-rate request carries no expense data. Your choice to switch scanning on is not used as a legal ground for these transfers under Turkish law. You can ask us for details at tembrisstudio@gmail.com.

7. How long data is kept

  • Data on your device: until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. We cannot recover it, because we never receive it. The temporary copy of a receipt photo is deleted right after text recognition. "Delete all data" in Settings erases the database content, settings, the PIN, scheduled notifications and temporary export and import files, and the app also removes leftover temporary files when it starts.
  • Free-trial status on your device (start date, clock record): kept until you uninstall the app. "Delete all data" keeps it (section 3), so that the trial cannot be restarted by erasing data.
  • Subscription status on your device: replaced on each check. "Delete all data" keeps it (section 3); uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Google (Play, Play Billing, ML Kit): under Google's own policies.
  • Frankfurter and its content-delivery provider: we receive and keep nothing from this request. How long the provider keeps technical logs is not stated by it.
  • Order data we see in Play Console: Google keeps it under its policies. A copy that we keep for our accounts is kept for the period that tax and accounting law requires.
  • E-mails to us: until your request is resolved and for 12 months afterwards, then deleted. A short log of requests (date, kind of request, outcome; no message content) is kept for 3 years to show that we answered.

8. Deleting your data and exercising your rights

  • Data in the app: open the app and delete expenses, categories and budgets, use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes everything the app stored on the device; "Delete all data" removes everything you created and keeps only the trial and subscription status records. Files you exported are outside our reach: delete them yourself.
  • Google data (Play, ML Kit): manage it through your Google account or Google's privacy tools. To stop the ML Kit metrics, switch receipt scanning off in Settings.
  • Order data we hold: write to tembrisstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". Please include the Google Play order number from your receipt e-mail so we can find your order. Deleting records does not cancel your subscription; cancel in Google Play (see the Terms). Some order data we must keep for tax and accounting law.
  • Exchange-rate service and GitHub: we hold no data from these requests. Contact them directly.

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, Turkish KVKK Law No. 6698 or California law) you may have the right to: be informed; access your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to processing; receive your data in a portable format; and withdraw consent where processing is consent-based. 10. Children

Kuruvo is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact tembrisstudio@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Security

Your data is kept in an encrypted database in the app's private storage. The database key is random, is created on your device and is kept in the Android Keystore. Android's sandboxing protects the app's storage from other apps. The optional app lock protects access to the app screens; while it is on, Android is told to block screenshots and screen recording and to blank the app preview in the recent-apps list. Notifications are set to hide their content on a locked screen. Network requests use HTTPS. The app checks Google's signature on each purchase before it grants premium. No system is perfectly secure, and you are responsible for protecting your device and any backup files you create.

If we learn of a personal data breach that concerns data we hold, we will notify the authorities and you as the law requires.

12. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera: only when you choose to scan a receipt and have switched scanning on. Photos are read on your device and not uploaded. Picking a photo from your gallery uses the system photo picker and needs no storage permission.
  • Notifications: only to show local reminders and alerts you enable.
  • Biometrics / device lock: only for the optional app lock.
  • Internet: for the exchange-rate request and for Google Play.
  • Run at start-up and vibration: to reschedule your reminders after a restart and to give haptic feedback.
  • The app asks for no access to your microphone, location, contacts, files or advertising ID.
