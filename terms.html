1. What Kuruvo is

Kuruvo is a personal expense tracker. You enter expenses (or scan receipts), organise them in categories, set budgets and see statistics. There is no account: everything you enter is stored on your own device, in an encrypted database. We cannot recover it if you lose the device, clear the app's data or uninstall the app, so make backups (the app can export a JSON or CSV file). If the database key is lost (for example after a device reset that removes the key), the data may not be readable any more; the app then offers to import a backup or to start fresh.
